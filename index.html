About the firm

We build modern structures - and the lives that happen inside them.

Founded in 1998 in Noida, Modern Structures is a full-service construction practice working across turnkey civil projects, interior design, façade design, and renovation. For nearly three decades, we have built a reputation on three things - quality, punctuality, and a client relationship that doesn't end at handover.

Every project, residential or commercial, gets a single point of contact, a fixed timeline, and a craftsmanship-first approach. From a foundation pour to the last cabinet finish, our teams stay on the work until the keys are in your hand.

"We don't quote, we commit. The site closes only when you're satisfied."

- The Founders, Modern Structures

How we work

Four steps, no surprises.

Our process is the same whether it's a single bedroom renovation or a turnkey home - predictable, paper-led, and walked through with you at every gate.

01

Consult

Site visit, brief, scope. We listen first, sketch second. No hard sell.

02

Design

3D renders, material boards, fixed BOQ. You sign off before a hammer is lifted.

03

Build

In-house crews, weekly site reviews, fortnightly photo updates. Always.

04

Handover

Snag-list closure, deep clean, walk-through, warranty. We close only when you're happy.
